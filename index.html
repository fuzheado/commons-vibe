About CommonsVibe

CommonsVibe is a visual explorer for Wikimedia Commons — a collection of over 100 million freely licensed images, videos, and sounds. Commons was built as a file warehouse; this tool turns it into a serendipitous, Pinterest-style feed you can wander through.

  • • Browse any category in a masonry feed — alphabetical or shuffled, detailed or minimal.
  • • Explore the category tree with the sitemap button: see subcategories to a chosen depth and jump between them.
  • • Deep shuffle samples an entire subtree at once — tiles are drawn from the category and all of its subcategories.
  • • Adjust tile size (S/M/L) to go from a dense thumbnail wall to big previews.
  • • Share what you see — the URL captures your category, trail, and settings, so any link opens the exact same view.
  • • Feed it a list — turn any PetScan query or PagePile into a browsable feed by adding a URL parameter: ?pet=<Category>&petdepth=N for a live PetScan subtree query, ?psid=<id> for a saved PetScan query, or ?pile=<id> for a PagePile. Run a PetScan search, hit "Create PagePile", and its number becomes a feed.

All media is pulled live from Commons and remains under its free license — image credits and file pages are one click away on every tile. Nothing is tracked, and nothing leaves your browser except API requests to Wikimedia.
